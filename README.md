<h1><strong>What is M risk?</strong></h1>
<p>M Risk is a Minecraft project where different kingdoms compete against each other. Each kingdom has subjects and kings. The difference between kings and subjects is that a subject has 1 heart, while kings have 2. The add-on only has a <span style="color:#f1c40f">German version</span>.</p>
<p>&nbsp;</p>
<h1><strong>Kings:</strong></h1>
<p>A king typically has two hearts. The first heart can be lost through any death. The second heart, however, can only be lost through an enemy king.</p>
<p>&nbsp;</p>
<h1><strong>Subject:</strong></h1>
<p>Subjects can only lose their hearts to an enemy fighter.</p>
<p>&nbsp;</p>
<h1><strong>Dummys and Combatlog</strong></h1>
<p>If a player logs out during a battle, a dummy is summoned to automatically <span style="color:#f1c40f">take over their items and hearts</span>. If this dummy dies, the actual player will also die when they log back in. If the dummy is not attacked and the combat log expires, the dummy will disappear automatically.</p>

<h1><strong>Nether/End</strong></h1>
<p>The Nether and End are locked by default. If you enter the Nether or End anyway, you will be teleported back to your spawn point. Other settings can be made under <span style="color:#f1c40f">/risiko-settings</span> and then under settings.</p>
<p>&nbsp;</p>
<p><img src="https://media.forgecdn.net/attachments/description/null/description_cd3221e1-eaf1-40cf-8e8b-9ce1e5ae9532.png" width="409" height="147"></p>
<p>&nbsp;</p>
<h1><strong>Create kingdom</strong></h1>
<p>To create a kingdom, first run the command <span style="color:#f1c40f">/risiko-settings</span> and then click on Kingdom. Here you can press the Add button to add a kingdom.</p>
<p>&nbsp;</p>

<p>&nbsp;</p>
<h1><strong>Manage kingdom</strong></h1>
<p>Once the kingdom has been created, you can add kings or subjects by clicking on the corresponding kingdom. Added members are displayed under "Members".</p>
